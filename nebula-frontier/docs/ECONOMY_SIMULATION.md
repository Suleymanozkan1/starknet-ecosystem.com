The simulation engine (`packages/economy/src/simulation.ts`) runs the **same policy functions as production**: `computeTreasuryHealth`, `computeEmissionRate` (hard cap) and `detectAnomalies` (circuit breakers + throttle). Monetary values are USD-equivalent; the reward asset is modelled in whole tokens with a simulated market price. Revenue is split per config: reward pool / treasury / operating / emergency reserves. Infrastructure + RPC costs are paid from operating cash first, then by selling treasury-reserve tokens — never from the reward pool.
