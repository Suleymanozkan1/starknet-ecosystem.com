# Economy Health Report

_Generated by `pnpm economy:simulate` on 2026-09-28 from economy.json defaults and the simulation in docs/ECONOMY_SIMULATION.md. Devnet only — tokens have no monetary value._

## Current Revenue Model

- **Premium currency (Gems)** bought with devnet SOL via verified on-chain deposits (`PREMIUM_REVENUE:SOL`). Gem packs and prices come from `shop.json` / `ShopProduct`.
- **Marketplace / auction / trade fees** (7.5% marketplace, 5.0% auction sale, 1.0% listing, 2.0% trade tax) in credits — act as sinks.
- **Withdrawal fees**: 2.0% + 0.001 NEBX flat + network fee → `FEE_REVENUE`.
- Premium/VIP/Elite, battle pass premium and cosmetics (no power sold for crypto).

## Reward Model

- Season reward budget = season revenue × 20.0% + reward-pool funding recorded from the **real** treasury balance. Never unlimited: no revenue and no funding ⇒ budget 0.
- Allocation: LEADERBOARD 30.0%, TOURNAMENT 25.0%, WORLD_EVENTS 15.0%, FACTION_WARS 10.0%, RAIDS 10.0%, ACHIEVEMENTS 5.0%, SPECIAL_CAMPAIGNS 5.0% (validated ≤ 100%).
- Emission rate = base 1.0% × activity × season × treasury-health multiplier × controller throttle, **hard-capped at 3.0%** of the budget per day.
- Per-player caps: 0.05 / 0.25 / 2 NEBX (day/week/season). Eligibility: 72h account age, 120 min play, 10 matches, risk ≤ MEDIUM, eligible modes only.
- Player-facing terms: Battle Rewards, Season Rewards, Tournament Rewards, Marketplace Earnings. Never APY / interest / guaranteed return / passive income / investment.

## Treasury Model

- Bootstrap reads the on-chain treasury balance and books only unaccounted funds, split: reward pool 20.0%, treasury reserve 40.0%, operating 30.0%, emergency 10.0%.
- Health = available reserve ÷ projected 30-day liabilities: HEALTHY ≥ 2, WATCH ≥ 1.5, WARNING ≥ 1, CRITICAL below; minimum reserve 10 NEBX.
- The economy controller (blockchain-service, every 5 min) snapshots supply/flows, detects anomalies and toggles circuit breakers with audit logs.

## Reward Liability

- Every grant creates a `RewardLiability` (OUTSTANDING → SETTLED on claim, EXPIRED/CANCELLED otherwise).
- **Invariant:** a grant is clipped so outstanding liabilities never exceed the `PLAYER_REWARD_POOL` balance (which itself only holds real, recorded funds). Verified by tests and by every simulated day in every scenario.
- Base growth, 100k users, 180 days: end outstanding liability 6.69 NEBX.

## Maximum Theoretical Liability

- Hard bound: outstanding liability ≤ reward pool balance (grant-time check), and daily new liability ≤ season budget × 3.0%.
- Cap bound (every eligible DAU maxes the daily cap): 100k users → 11,250 eligible DAU × 0.05 = 562.5 NEBX/day — but the emission hard cap and pool bound apply first, so actual liability is min(cap bound, budget × rate, pool).
- Per-player season maximum: 2 NEBX.

## Withdrawal Exposure

- Only claimed rewards (player NEBX balance) can be withdrawn; min 0.01, max 1, daily 2 NEBX per player, 60 min cooldown, 48h wallet-change lock, ≥ 0.5 NEBX → manual review.
- Minimum withdrawal quote: requested 10000000, service fee 1200000, network fee 5000, final 8795000 lamports.
- Bank-run scenario (HIGH_WITHDRAWAL, 100k, 180d): withdrawals $35.6k, breaker days 0, liability exceeded pool: never.
- Funds are held in `WITHDRAWAL_RESERVE` at request time; COMPLETED only after on-chain confirmation; failures refund principal + fees.

## Inflation Risk

- Credits (minted soft currency): controller watches daily/weekly inflation (targets 2.0% / 8.0%; spike 5.0% ⇒ EVENT_PAUSE + reward throttle ×0.8).
- NEBX: fixed max supply 1,000,000,000, no infinite mint; rewards are distributed from a funded pool, so "inflation" is distribution of existing supply (base growth 100k/180d avg 1.8% per day of player-held balance).

## Bot Risk

- Heuristics: repeated reward extraction, metronome-regular intervals, account clustering by device fingerprint / IP / wallet relationships (deposits & withdrawals). Signals raise risk score → HIGH/CRITICAL ⇒ rewards PENDING_REVIEW, withdrawals to manual review. Never an automatic permanent ban.
- Bot-wave scenario (HIGH_BOT_ACTIVITY, 100k, 180d): reward expense $19.2k, breaker days 73; circuit breaker REWARD_PAUSE engages when > 15.0% of rewarded users are high-risk.

## Market Risk

- Rewards and liabilities are denominated in the reward asset, so a price crash does not break reserve coverage; it raises the token cost of fiat-denominated infrastructure. MARKET_CRASH (100k, 180d): treasury $149.8k, net margin $167.3k, min coverage 8.96.
- MARKET_PAUSE breaker exists for marketplace manipulation (volume spike with > 50% from a single seller).
